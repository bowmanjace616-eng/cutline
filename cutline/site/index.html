Questions

What kind of videos does it work best on?

Anything where someone is talking: talking-head YouTube videos, tutorials, podcasts, vlogs, reviews. Cutline uses the transcript to decide what to cut and which moments make good Shorts.

Will it ruin my original footage?

No. Every edit is a list of instructions over your original file. Nothing is re-encoded until you render, and you can undo any change.

How long does it take?

A 10-minute video usually takes a few minutes for the first cut and Shorts. AI scene edits take a few minutes per clip because the new footage is generated.

How much does it cost?

Free during the beta. AI scene edits use paid video generation, so those will be metered when paid plans launch.

Do I need Claude to use it?

No. The web app works on its own. If you already use Claude, you can also add Cutline as a connector and edit from your chat.
